New to momos?

Nepal's favorite dumpling

Momos are dumplings from Nepal and the Himalayas, filled with seasoned chicken or vegetables and served with chutney for dipping.

Every style comes in chicken or veg. Not sure where to start? Get the steamed momo first, then try the chili momo next time.

Good to know

Questions

Craving momos or chow mein in Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Santa Clara or Mountain View? Here’s what people usually ask.

Where is the Asian Momo House food truck?

We park at 153 E Fremont Ave in Sunnyvale, CA 94087, a short drive from Cupertino, Santa Clara and Mountain View.

What are your hours?

We’re open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:30 AM to 10 PM. We’re closed on Mondays.

What are momos?

Momos are Nepali dumplings from the Himalayas, filled with seasoned chicken or vegetables and served with chutney. We make them steamed, chili, kothey (pan-fried), fried and jhol (in a warm, tangy broth). A plate is nine pieces.

Do you have vegetarian food?

Yes. Every momo style comes in veg, and we also make veg and schezwan chow mein, veg fried rice, paneer chili, gobi manchurian and veg manchurian.

Can I order online for pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order on DoorDash or Uber Eats, or call the truck at (408) 900-8359.

Do you cater parties or office lunches?

Yes. We make half and full party trays of momos, chow mein, fried rice and Indo-Chinese dishes. A full momo tray is 110 pieces. Call (408) 900-8359 to order.
