New to momos?

Nepal's favorite dumpling

Momos are dumplings from Nepal and the Himalayas, filled with seasoned chicken or vegetables and served with chutney for dipping.

Every style comes in chicken or veg. Not sure where to start? Get the steamed momo first, then try the chili momo next time.

  • SteamedSoft, juicy, and classic.
  • ChiliTossed in a spicy, saucy chili glaze.
  • KotheyPan-fried with a crisp golden bottom.
  • JholServed in a warm, tangy broth.
