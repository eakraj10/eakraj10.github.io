ABOUT US

Asian Momo House

Asian Momo House is a family-owned food truck in Sunnyvale, California, serving Nepali momos and Indo-Chinese cuisine.

Our menu includes steamed and chili momos, chow mein, fried rice, and vegetarian and chicken dishes. Visit our truck or order delivery through DoorDash or Uber Eats.
